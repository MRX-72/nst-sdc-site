Who we are

        

          
The NST Student Developer Club is a community of developers, designers and tinkerers, run entirely by students of Newton School of Technology. We give students a place to collaborate, learn and grow together in tech.

          
Our mission is to build a culture of collaboration, innovation and open-source contribution, so every member leaves ready for real-world engineering.

        

      


      
What we do

      
      

        

          
Open-source projects

          
We build and maintain tools that NST students actually use, all in the open on GitHub. Every October, Hacktoberfest events and beginner workshops take members from zero to their first merged pull request.

        

        

          
Workshops and webinars

          
Hands-on sessions on the tools the industry uses right now, from Go and LangChain to Docker, Kubernetes and DevOps pipelines.

        

        

          
Competitions and hackathons

          
Timed builds and regular coding challenges, from terminal puzzles in Quicksnatch to full hackathons, where you solve real problems under pressure and then demo what you made.

        

        

          
Mentorship and GSoC prep

          
Freshers pair with senior members, get their code reviewed and learn how production software is really built. Those aiming for Google Summer of Code get focused help contributing to open-source organisations first.

        

        

          
Knowledge sharing

          
Docs, tutorials and a project ideas library with 64 guided builds, so what one member learns, everyone can use. Members showcase what they've built and swap ideas with each other.

Newton School of Technology

          
Where the club lives.

        

        

          
Newton School of Technology (NST) was founded in 2023 by IIT Roorkee alumni Siddharth Maheshwari and Nishant Chandra. It runs a four-year, residential B.Tech in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, recognised by the UGC and AICTE.

          
The curriculum is project-first. Students build systems like recommendation engines and delivery optimisers from year one, taught by engineers who have worked at companies like Google and Goldman Sachs. The club exists for everything that happens after class.

Get involved

      
Two ways in. Members join the club; contributors can help from anywhere, no membership needed.

      

        

          
As a member

          
    
            
  1. 1
    Join us. Leave your email below and follow our channels for recruitment and events.
    2. 
            
  2. 2
    Collaborate. Pick up an ongoing project or pitch your own idea.
    3. 
            
  3. 3
    Learn and grow. Show up to workshops, events and mentorship sessions.
    4. 
          

        

        

          
As a contributor

          
    
            
  1. 1
    Explore. Browse our repos and look for good first issues.
    2. 
            
  2. 2
    Fork and open a PR. Follow each repo's contribution guidelines and send your change.
    3. 
            
  3. 3
    Collaborate. Talk it through with maintainers in the PR and help us improve.
    4. 
          

        

      


      
      

        

          Do I need experience to join?+
          
No. Freshers pair with senior members from day one, and our project ideas library starts from "an input and a button".

        

        

          Can I contribute if I'm not at NST?+
          
Yes. Our code is public. Fork any repo, pick an issue and open a pull request.

        

        

          What will I actually work on?+
          
Web apps, mobile apps, AI/ML and DevOps. Mostly tools that solve real problems on campus, like the ones listed above.

        

        

          How do I reach the club?+
          
Email support@nstsdc.org, or message us on LinkedIn, X or Instagram. Links are at the bottom of the page.
